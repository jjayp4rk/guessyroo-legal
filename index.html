Guessyroo Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 5, 2026

Guessyroo is a kids' guessing game made by Nakatech. This policy is simple because the app is simple: Guessyroo does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

What the app stores

Game progress, settings, and custom decks you create are saved only on your device. They are never uploaded anywhere and are deleted when you delete the app.

What the app does NOT do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No ads
  • No analytics or tracking
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data
  • No access to contacts, photos, location, camera, or microphone

In-app purchases

Guessyroo offers a single one-time purchase to unlock all decks. Payment is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store; we never see or store payment details. The purchase button sits behind a parental gate.

Children's privacy

Guessyroo is designed for families with young children. Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children, in line with COPPA and similar regulations.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices ever change, this page will be updated and the effective date revised before the change ships.
