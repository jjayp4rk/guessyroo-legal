From “what shall we do?”
to “my turn!”

  1. 1

    Pick your pictures.

    Start with Animals or Food. Both decks are free, every time you play.

  2. 2

    Give clues. Get silly.

    Face the phone toward your family and guess their clues. Or take turns acting out the pictures.

  3. 3

    Pass the phone.

    Score a guess, skip a tricky one, and give the next player a turn. Choose a timer or practice at your own pace.

A game that grows
with your family.

Use pictures for little players, words for readers, or both together. Make your own free word decks for spelling lists, favorite things, or family jokes.
